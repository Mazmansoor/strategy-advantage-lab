Strategy Advantage Lab helps leadership teams examine difficult strategic questions, weigh meaningful alternatives, and turn choices into accountable action.

The problem

More information doesn't always create more clarity.

Critical strategic discussions can stall because teams disagree about the actual problem, rely on different assumptions, or hesitate to acknowledge the trade-offs involved. The answer is not necessarily another presentation. Sometimes it is a more rigorous conversation about the decision itself.

The Strategy Advantage Sprint

A disciplined four-week engagement.

01 / DISCOVER

Find the real strategic question.

Understand the decision, stakeholders, available evidence, and competing interpretations.

02 / CHALLENGE

Test assumptions and alternatives.

Make uncertainty visible. Examine options, risks, constraints, and opportunity costs.

03 / CHOOSE

Clarify strategic trade-offs.

Support authorized leaders in examining criteria, priorities, and choices—including what not to do.

04 / ACTIVATE

Turn choices into action.

Define owners, first milestones, review triggers, and a practical 90-day action roadmap.

The takeaways

A decision your team can work with.

The engagement culminates in a Strategic Decision Dossier, a concise working record of the strategic question, assumptions, options, trade-offs, agreed decisions or open issues, accountability, and a 90-day roadmap.

The Lab facilitates disciplined thinking and practical planning. Client leaders retain responsibility for their decisions and implementation. This is not a promise of guaranteed consensus, revenue, or organizational outcomes.

Good fit

Designed for decisions that matter.

  • An executive or division team is facing an important unresolved choice.
  • An authorized sponsor can participate and involve necessary colleagues.
  • The problem is bounded enough for a focused engagement.
  • The organization intends to act on what it learns.
