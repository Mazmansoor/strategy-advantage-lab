Learning by deciding

A gym for strategic judgment. Not another course of slides.

Most training explains what good strategy looks like. Here, you make a choice before the answer is clear. You defend your reasoning. You encounter consequences. And you learn to take a second look.

01 / SEE

Question your first interpretation

Distinguish what you know from what you've assumed. Explore missing perspectives and competing explanations.

02 / CHOOSE

Make the difficult tradeoff

Decide with limited resources, uncertain outcomes, and legitimate competing priorities.

03 / RECONSIDER

Learn when the world changes

Respond to new evidence without confusing intellectual humility with indecision.

The philosophy
The advantage isn't knowing the future. It's knowing how to think when you don't.

Built on the ideas in The Strategy Advantage, the Lab transforms strategic thinking into a practice: See → Interpret → Choose → Align → Act → Measure → Learn → Reconsider.

Developed for curious professionals, aspiring leaders, and teams seeking more thoughtful decisions—not a magic score or a predetermined “right answer.”

Practice with a second look

A thinking companion that challenges your reasoning.

In the prototype, a transparent rules-based Socratic coach examines the reasons and assumptions you type, then asks follow-up questions in your Strategic Reasoning Review. A future version can connect a real AI model to create richer, context-aware dialogue while keeping learning evidence private and under participant control.

01 / QUESTION

What do you really know?

Separate observations from interpretations and examine the weakest evidence.

02 / STRETCH

What are you missing?

Consider neglected stakeholders, dependencies, and second-order effects.

03 / REVISIT

What would change your mind?

Specify meaningful evidence before declaring a strategic position permanent.
