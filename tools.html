The Strategic Decision Studio

Get a difficult decision out of your head and into a disciplined working record. Follow five stages, uncover assumptions, consider alternatives, and leave with next steps.

Privacy by design: Your responses stay in this browser session. The tool does not transmit or save your answers to our servers. You can download a text record. Avoid entering confidential or sensitive information.

01 — Notice

What is happening—and what might we be missing?

Describe the situation before prescribing a solution.

Prompt: What would a thoughtful skeptic notice that our team has overlooked?
02 — Frame

What decision must actually be made?

Test: If a different root cause were true, would your decision question change?
03 — Choose

What are the genuine alternatives?

Do not use false numerical precision: a weighted score cannot replace accountable judgment.
04 — Act

What happens in the next 90 days?

05 — Learn

What would make us change our minds?

A decision is a working hypothesis. Good leadership includes knowing when to revise it.
